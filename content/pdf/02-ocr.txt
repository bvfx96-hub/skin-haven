What Are Acne Scars?
Book Now
Acne scars develop when there is an uneven collagen production response after
experiencing moderate to severe acne breakouts. While mild acne might lead to temporary
hyperpigmentation, it generally fades naturally with time. TO truly diminish the visibility of
acne scars, medical treatment is essential. Home remedies and over-the-counter products
lack substantial evidence of their effectiveness and safety, and they may even aggravate
acne-prone skin, potentially causing further harm.
Types of Acne Scars?
Numerous varieties of acne scars exist, each displaying distinctive characteristics, and only a
dermatologist possesses the expertise to accurately discern them. These encompass rolling
scars, boxcar scars, ice pick scars, macular erythematous scars, and keloids. Additionally,
mild acne can lead to the formation of acne marks or post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation,
which may or may not diminish with time.
