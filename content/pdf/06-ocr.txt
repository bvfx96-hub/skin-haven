What is dermal filler treatment?
Dermal fillers, sometimes referred to as injectable implants, soft tissue fillers, lip and facial
enhancements, or wrinkle fillers, are medical device implants authorized by the US FDA. They
are used to achieve a smoother or more voluminous appearance in various facial areas,
such as the nasolabial folds (the lines running from the sides of the nose to the corners of the
mouth), cheeks, chin, lips, and the back of the hands.

hyaluronic acid in our bodies resides in the skin. Its primary function is to retain moisture and

complications. The risks associated with these fillers are akin to those accompanying any
other type of injection.
@ Bruising may occur in certain cases, typically resolving within 1-2 days.
@ Swelling may sornetimes develop at the injection site but tends to subside within 1-2
days if it does occur.
@ Individuals with bleeding disorders like hemophilia should avoid derrnal fillers.
@ In the rare instance of filler injection into a blood vessel, complications may arise.
However, this risk is significantly reduced when administered by a skilled injector.
These considerations underscore the importance of having your fillers performed exclusively
under the guidance of a highly qualified dermatologist. They can effectively minimize these
risks and provide you with guidance on how to manage them, although the chances of their
occurrence are minimal.
