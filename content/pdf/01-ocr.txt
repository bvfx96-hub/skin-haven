Acne, a skin condition, arises when pores become clogged due to a combination of hair,
sebum, bacteria, and dead skin cells. These obstructions can manifest as various types of
pimples, including blackheads, whiteheads, nodules, and more. commonly referred to as
pimples or blemishes, they often appear on the face, chest, shoulders, neck, back (often
termed "bacne"), buttocks, forehead, and nose. It's a prevalent issue, with estimates
indicating that approximately 80% of individuals aged II to 30 will encounter a mild form of
acne at some point in their lives. Many people will experience acne at some stage, and the
most prevalent form is known as Acne vulgaris.
Causes Of Acne
Acne primarily arises from hormonal imbalances, specifically an excess of androgen
hormones, which are most active during adolescence and early adulthood. When combined
with the presence of skin surface bacteria and the production of fatty acids by oil glands,
acne can manifest.

Where does acne most commonly occur?
Acne typically appears in several common areas, including your face, forehead, chest,
shoulders, and upper back. Back acne is often referred to as "bacne." In addition to these
areas, acne can also occur in various other places such as buttocks (referred to as butt acne
or bum acne), other parts of the body, hands, scrotum, breasts, anus, small bumps on the
nose, and in the vicinity of the vagina. Oil glands are distributed throughout your body, but
these are the areas where they tend to be most concentrated. The most effective approach
to treating acne depends on its severity.

@ Bacterial Acne: This is the most prevalent type of acne, characterized by a bacterial
infection that leads to the development of papules, pustules, and cysts.
@ Fungal Acne: This form of acne results from an infection by fungus or yeast within the
acne lesions.
@ Hormonal Acne: Hormonal imbalances, particularly an excess of male hormones or
androgens, are the primary causes of hormonal acne. Examples include PCOD Acne and
Adolescent acne.
@ Comedogenic Acne: Acne that features comedones, namely
whiteheads, is referred to as comedogenic acne.
