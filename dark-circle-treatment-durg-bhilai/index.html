Dark circles and under-eye bags can make the face appear tired, dull or older even after adequate rest. These concerns may affect people of different ages and can develop due to multiple factors rather than a single cause.

At Skin Haven Clinic, under-eye concerns are carefully assessed before recommending skincare or aesthetic treatment. Patients from Durg, Bhilai, Risali, Supela, Nehru Nagar and nearby areas can consult for personalised under-eye care.

Common Causes of Dark Circles

  • Genetics and family history
  • Thin under-eye skin
  • Increased pigmentation
  • Lack of sleep and fatigue
  • Allergies and frequent eye rubbing
  • Sun exposure
  • Dehydration
  • Stress and lifestyle factors
  • Age-related volume loss
  • Under-eye hollows or tear troughs

Dark Circles and Eye Bags Are Different

Dark circles generally appear as brown, grey, blue or purple discolouration beneath the eyes. Under-eye bags are caused by swelling, puffiness or structural changes. Some patients may have both concerns simultaneously.

Because the underlying causes can differ, the same treatment may not be suitable for everyone.

Dermatologist-Guided Treatment

Following a skin and under-eye assessment, care may include appropriate skincare, sun protection, lifestyle guidance, pigmentation-focused procedures or other suitable aesthetic options.

The dermatologist will consider skin sensitivity, pigmentation depth, facial structure, age and overall medical history before suggesting treatment.

Daily Under-Eye Care

  • Get adequate sleep
  • Avoid rubbing the eyes
  • Manage allergies appropriately
  • Use sunscreen around the eye area as advised
  • Avoid harsh or unverified bleaching products
  • Maintain hydration
  • Follow dermatologist-recommended skincare

Frequently Asked Questions

Can dark circles disappear completely?

Improvement depends on their cause. Genetic or structural dark circles may require ongoing management.

Do creams work for every type of dark circle?

No. Creams may help certain pigmentation-related concerns but may not correct hollowness or prominent under-eye bags.

Are dark circles caused only by lack of sleep?

No. Genetics, allergies, pigmentation, ageing and facial structure may also contribute.

Is under-eye treatment safe?

Treatment should be selected and performed by a qualified professional after proper assessment.
