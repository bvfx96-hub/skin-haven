Stretch marks and scars are common changes in the skin that may develop after pregnancy, growth, weight changes, acne, injury or surgery. Although they are usually harmless, some people may seek treatment to make them less noticeable or improve the skin’s texture.

Skin Haven Clinic provides personalised evaluation of stretch marks and different types of scars for patients from Durg, Bhilai and nearby Chhattisgarh locations.

Why Do Stretch Marks Develop?

Stretch marks can appear when the skin stretches or changes rapidly. They commonly occur during:

  • Pregnancy
  • Puberty and growth spurts
  • Rapid weight gain or loss
  • Muscle gain
  • Hormonal changes
  • Long-term use of certain steroid medications
  • Genetic predisposition

Newer stretch marks may appear red, purple or pink, while older marks generally become lighter or white.

Types of Scars

  • Acne scars
  • Surgical scars
  • Injury scars
  • Burn scars
  • Raised or hypertrophic scars
  • Keloids
  • Depressed scars
  • Dark or pigmented scars

Different scar types need different treatment plans. A treatment that works for a flat pigmented scar may not be appropriate for a raised keloid.

Treatment Approach

After evaluating the type, age, location and size of the scar or stretch mark, treatment may include:

  • Prescription topical care
  • Silicone-based scar care when suitable
  • Chemical peels
  • Microneedling
  • Laser-based scar procedures
  • Treatment for raised scars
  • Combination procedures
  • Maintenance skincare and sun protection

Treatment can often make scars or stretch marks less noticeable, but complete removal cannot always be guaranteed.

Avoid Unsupervised Treatments

Aggressive scrubbing, home remedies and unverified creams may cause irritation or pigmentation. Keloids and changing scars should be assessed before treatment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can stretch marks disappear completely?

They may fade naturally and treatment can make them less noticeable, but complete removal may not be possible.

Are newer stretch marks easier to treat?

Newer marks may respond differently from older white stretch marks. Early evaluation can help determine suitable options.

Can laser remove a scar permanently?

Laser treatment may reduce a scar’s visibility but generally does not erase it completely.

How many sessions are required?

The number of sessions depends on the scar type, age, depth, location and selected procedure.
