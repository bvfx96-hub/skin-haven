Hair fall can occur temporarily or progress gradually over time. While losing some hair daily is normal, excessive shedding, widening of the hair partition, receding hairline or visible bald patches may require dermatological evaluation.

Skin Haven Clinic provides diagnosis-based care for hair loss and scalp concerns for men and women across Durg, Bhilai and nearby Chhattisgarh areas.

Common Signs of Hair Loss

  • Excessive hair shedding
  • Hair thinning
  • Widening centre partition
  • Receding hairline
  • Bald patches
  • Reduced hair density
  • Weak or easily breakable hair
  • Hair loss after illness or stress
  • Scalp itching, flaking or inflammation

Possible Causes of Hair Fall

  • Genetic or pattern hair loss
  • Nutritional deficiencies
  • Hormonal changes
  • Thyroid-related conditions
  • Stress
  • Recent fever, illness or surgery
  • Pregnancy or childbirth
  • Significant weight loss
  • Certain medicines
  • Dandruff or scalp conditions
  • Alopecia areata
  • Tight hairstyles and chemical treatments

Why Proper Diagnosis Matters

Hair loss treatment depends on its underlying cause. Random supplements, oils or salon procedures may not help if the condition is caused by genetics, hormones, inflammation or an underlying health issue.

The dermatologist may examine the scalp, review medical history and recommend investigations when required.

Personalised Hair-Loss Treatment

Depending on the diagnosis, treatment may include:

  • Prescription scalp medication
  • Hair-growth solutions
  • Nutritional correction when required
  • Dandruff or scalp treatment
  • Hair PRP therapy when suitable
  • Additional dermatologist-recommended procedures
  • Long-term maintenance planning

No treatment can guarantee identical results for every person. Early diagnosis may help preserve existing hair and improve treatment planning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is all hair fall permanent?

No. Some forms are temporary, while genetic or scarring hair loss may be progressive.

Does PRP work for everyone?

PRP may be suitable for selected patients. A dermatologist must first assess the type and stage of hair loss.

Can oil cure genetic baldness?

Oils may condition the hair but do not generally reverse genetic hair loss.

When should I consult a dermatologist?

Seek evaluation for sudden shedding, bald patches, scalp symptoms or continuously decreasing hair density.
