Dermal fillers are injectable treatments used for selected volume and contour concerns. A consultation at Skin Haven in Durg helps patients from Bhilai and Chhattisgarh understand whether a filler-based approach fits their goals.

What fillers do

Some fillers contain hyaluronic acid and can add volume in selected facial areas. Product choice, the area being treated and individual anatomy matter. Fillers are medical procedures, not routine salon treatments.

Topics for your consultation

  • Volume loss and facial proportions
  • Lip or cheek concerns
  • Previous fillers and other aesthetic procedures
  • Medical history, medicines and allergies
  • Expected duration, review visits and alternatives

Preparation and aftercare

Discuss your medicines and supplements without stopping prescribed treatment on your own. Your clinician should explain the product, procedure, expected swelling and review plan. Follow the specific instructions given for your treatment.

Important risks

Bruising, swelling, infection, lumps and asymmetry may occur. Rarely, accidental injection into a blood vessel can cause tissue damage, vision loss or stroke. Severe or unusual pain, skin colour change or a vision disturbance needs urgent medical assessment.

Frequently asked questions

Are results permanent?

Many fillers are temporary. Duration varies by product, location and individual response.

Are under-eye fillers suitable for everyone?

No. Under-eye anatomy and the cause of the concern require careful assessment.

Can I choose treatment from a photograph?

A photograph can help explain your goals, but an examination is needed to judge what is appropriate.
