A thread lift uses threads beneath the skin to support selected tissue and address certain laxity concerns. Skin Haven offers a consultation-led discussion for patients considering facial rejuvenation in Durg–Bhilai.

Understanding thread lifts

Thread lifts differ from a surgical facelift. They may be discussed for selected patients with suitable skin and expectations, but they cannot reproduce every surgical result or permanently stop ageing.

What determines suitability

  • The degree and location of skin laxity
  • Skin quality and facial structure
  • Previous procedures and medical history
  • Your expectations, recovery time and willingness to attend review visits

The treatment discussion

Your clinician should explain the thread type, the intended effect, alternatives and the expected recovery. Treatment planning is individual; the number or brand of threads used by a different clinic does not determine your plan.

Risks and recovery

Bruising, swelling, pain, infection, visible or palpable threads and thread extrusion can occur. Results may be uneven or less noticeable than expected. Follow aftercare instructions and contact the clinic for persistent pain or other concerning changes.

Frequently asked questions

Is a thread lift permanent?

No. Results vary and are not permanent.

Is it the same as a facelift?

No. A surgical facelift and a thread lift differ in scope, recovery and expected effects.

Is there downtime?

Recovery varies. Discuss time away from work or social activities before scheduling.
