Acne is one of the most common skin concerns affecting teenagers and adults. It may appear as blackheads, whiteheads, red pimples, painful bumps, cysts or pus-filled lesions. Without appropriate care, frequent or severe acne may leave dark marks, uneven texture and permanent scars.

At Skin Haven – Skin, Hair, Aesthetic & Laser Clinic, patients from Durg, Bhilai and nearby areas of Chhattisgarh receive dermatologist-guided acne care based on their skin type, age, acne severity and possible triggering factors.

What Causes Acne?

Acne develops when skin pores become blocked by excess oil, dead skin cells and inflammation. Hormonal changes, genetics, stress, unsuitable cosmetics, certain medicines, dietary habits and humid weather may contribute to acne breakouts.

The warm and humid climate of Durg-Bhilai can also increase sweating and oiliness, making acne-prone skin more difficult to manage without a suitable skincare routine.

Types of Acne We Evaluate

  • Blackheads and whiteheads
  • Red and inflamed pimples
  • Hormonal acne
  • Teenage acne
  • Adult acne
  • Cystic and nodular acne
  • Back, chest and shoulder acne
  • Acne associated with oily skin
  • Acne marks and post-inflammatory pigmentation
  • Ice-pick, rolling and boxcar acne scars

Personalised Acne Treatment

Treatment is selected after examining the patient’s skin and identifying the type and severity of acne. Depending on individual requirements, the dermatologist may recommend prescription skincare, chemical peels, comedone management or suitable clinical procedures.

Active acne is usually controlled before beginning advanced scar treatments. This helps reduce inflammation and may prevent new scars from developing.

Acne Scar Treatment Options

Different scars require different approaches. Treatment may include dermatologist-recommended procedures such as chemical peels, microneedling, laser-based procedures or combination therapy. Results and the number of sessions vary according to the scar type, skin condition and healing response.

Why Avoid Self-Treatment?

Squeezing or picking pimples may increase inflammation, infection, pigmentation and scarring. Strong creams or steroid-containing products used without medical advice may irritate or damage the skin.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can acne be treated permanently?

Acne can usually be controlled effectively, but recurrence depends on hormones, genetics, skincare and other individual factors.

How long does acne treatment take?

Visible improvement may require several weeks. The treatment duration depends on the type and severity of acne.

Can old acne scars be treated?

Many acne scars can be made less noticeable, although complete removal cannot always be guaranteed.

When should I consult a dermatologist?

Consult a dermatologist when acne is painful, persistent, spreading, leaving marks or affecting your confidence.
