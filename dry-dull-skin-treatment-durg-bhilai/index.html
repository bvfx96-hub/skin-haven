Dry skin may feel tight, rough, flaky, itchy or uncomfortable. Dull skin may appear tired, uneven or lacking in freshness. These concerns can occur together but may have different underlying causes.

Seasonal weather changes, air conditioning, hot showers, harsh cleansers, dehydration and unsuitable skincare products can affect the skin barrier.

Common Symptoms

  • Tightness after washing
  • Flaking or peeling
  • Rough skin texture
  • Itching or irritation
  • Reduced softness
  • Dull or tired appearance
  • Fine dehydration lines
  • Sensitive or easily irritated skin
  • Uneven makeup application

What Causes Dry and Dull Skin?

Possible factors include:

  • Harsh soaps or face washes
  • Frequent exfoliation
  • Inadequate moisturisation
  • Hot water
  • Weather changes
  • Sun exposure
  • Age-related changes
  • Certain medicines
  • Eczema or other skin conditions
  • Poorly selected cosmetic products

Personalised Skin Assessment

Persistent dryness should not always be treated as a cosmetic concern. Eczema, allergies, thyroid-related conditions and other skin disorders may also cause dryness or itching.

A dermatologist can evaluate the condition and recommend a suitable skin-barrier repair plan.

Treatment and Skin-Rejuvenation Options

Depending on individual needs, care may include:

  • Gentle cleansing recommendations
  • Suitable moisturisers
  • Barrier-repair skincare
  • Sunscreen
  • Hydrating facials
  • HydraFacial-type procedures
  • Chemical peels when appropriate
  • Skin-rejuvenation procedures
  • Treatment of underlying skin conditions

Daily Care Tips

  • Avoid excessively hot water
  • Use gentle, fragrance-free products when required
  • Apply moisturiser soon after washing
  • Avoid frequent scrubbing
  • Use sunscreen regularly
  • Do not overuse active ingredients
  • Maintain adequate hydration

Frequently Asked Questions

Is dry skin caused only by dehydration?

No. Weather, skincare products, medical conditions and damage to the skin barrier may also contribute.

Can oily skin also be dehydrated?

Yes. Oily skin can still lack water and feel dehydrated.

Will a facial permanently cure dull skin?

A facial may improve appearance temporarily. Long-term results require suitable skincare and management of the underlying cause.

When should dry skin be medically evaluated?

Consult a dermatologist if dryness is persistent, severely itchy, cracked, inflamed or spreading.
