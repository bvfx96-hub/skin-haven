Fine lines and wrinkles are natural changes that occur as the skin ages. Over time, the skin produces less collagen and elastin, leading to reduced firmness, dryness and visible lines.

Sun exposure, facial expressions, smoking, stress, inadequate skincare and environmental factors may make these changes appear earlier or become more noticeable.

Common Ageing Concerns

  • Forehead lines
  • Frown lines
  • Crow’s feet around the eyes
  • Smile lines
  • Fine lines around the mouth
  • Loss of skin firmness
  • Uneven texture
  • Dull or tired-looking skin
  • Sun-related skin ageing
  • Enlarged pores

Personalised Anti-Ageing Assessment

Anti-ageing treatment should not follow a one-size-fits-all approach. The dermatologist considers the patient’s age, facial structure, skin type, line depth, medical history and desired outcome.

The goal is to support healthier, refreshed and natural-looking skin without creating an artificial appearance.

Possible Treatment Options

Based on individual assessment, care may include:

  • Medical-grade skincare
  • Sunscreen and preventive skincare
  • Chemical peels
  • Microneedling
  • Skin-rejuvenation procedures
  • Laser-based procedures
  • Other dermatologist-recommended aesthetic treatments

Treatment suitability, recovery period and expected improvement are discussed during consultation.

Preventing Premature Skin Ageing

  • Use broad-spectrum sunscreen regularly
  • Avoid excessive sun exposure
  • Maintain a gentle skincare routine
  • Avoid smoking
  • Get adequate sleep
  • Stay hydrated
  • Do not use harsh anti-ageing products without guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

At what age should anti-ageing skincare begin?

Preventive skincare and sun protection can begin in early adulthood. Procedure requirements depend on individual concerns.

Can wrinkles be removed completely?

Treatments may soften wrinkles and improve texture, but complete or permanent removal cannot always be promised.

Will the face look artificial after treatment?

Appropriate treatment aims for subtle and natural-looking improvement.

How many sessions are needed?

The number depends on the selected procedure, skin condition and desired result.
