Pigmentation refers to patches or areas of skin that appear darker than the surrounding complexion. It may affect the face, neck, hands or other body areas and can occur due to excess melanin production, inflammation, sun exposure, hormonal changes or skin injury.

Skin Haven Clinic provides dermatologist-guided care for pigmentation, melasma, post-acne marks and uneven skin tone in Durg, Bhilai and nearby areas of Chhattisgarh.

Types of Pigmentation Concerns

  • Melasma
  • Post-acne dark marks
  • Sunspots and age spots
  • Uneven skin tone
  • Tanning
  • Freckles
  • Pigmentation around the mouth
  • Underarm pigmentation
  • Neck pigmentation
  • Pigmentation after injury or inflammation

Why Does Pigmentation Occur?

Common contributing factors include prolonged sun exposure, hormonal changes, pregnancy, acne, friction, skin irritation, inappropriate cosmetic products and certain medical conditions.

Durg-Bhilai’s strong sunlight and warm weather may increase tanning and worsen existing pigmentation when suitable sun protection is not followed.

Importance of Correct Diagnosis

Different pigmentation conditions can look similar but may require different treatment approaches. Using fairness creams or strong combination creams without medical supervision can irritate the skin and sometimes worsen pigmentation.

A dermatologist evaluates the pattern, depth, duration and possible trigger before developing an individual treatment plan.

Available Treatment Approach

Depending on the diagnosis, treatment may involve:

  • Prescription skincare
  • Daily sunscreen guidance
  • Chemical peels
  • Pigmentation-focused procedures
  • Suitable laser-based procedures
  • Acne management
  • Maintenance skincare

Pigmentation generally requires consistency and ongoing sun protection. Results vary according to the condition, skin type and depth of pigmentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can pigmentation return after treatment?

Some forms, including melasma, may recur. Maintenance care and sun protection are important.

How long does pigmentation treatment take?

Improvement is gradual and may require several weeks or months depending on its type and severity.

Can salon facials permanently remove pigmentation?

Facials may temporarily improve brightness but may not address the underlying cause of pigmentation.

Should I use fairness or steroid creams?

Do not use strong combination or steroid creams without a dermatologist’s advice.
